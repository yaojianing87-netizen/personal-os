---
name: Notes and Inspiration roles
description: Product boundary between long-term structured notes and quick inspiration capture.
---

Notes and Inspiration are separate modules. Notes are for long-term, longer-form writing organized in nested folders; Inspiration is for quick ideas and temporary information without folders.

**Why:** The user defined these as distinct parts of the Personal OS and asked that they not be merged.

**How to apply:** Keep their interfaces and persisted data independent. Add hierarchy only to Notes; keep Inspiration lightweight.
